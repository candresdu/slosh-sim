# Outer-loop margins and gain-shift threshold

Generated by `python -m slosh.design.margins` from the linearized nonlinear model
(`slosh.design.linearize`): central-difference Jacobian about hover, ZOH at 100 Hz, inner PD loop closed, outer loop broken at `u_a` (output of the outer PD, GS = K_T = 1).

PD gains are *designed (reconstruction)* values from `slosh.design.fit_gains` and the motor time constant is assumed; see `shared/params/default.json` for provenance.

- Outer PD: Kp = 1.092, Kd = 0.8717
- Inner PD: Kp = 13.02, Kd = 3.473
- Motor time constant: 40 ms (assumed)
- Slosh mode (open loop, undamped): 3.183 rad/s = 0.507 Hz

Phase margin and gain margins use the discrete loop `L(z)` evaluated up to the Nyquist frequency. A lower gain margin (phase crossing with |L| > 1) would mean a *low* gain also destabilizes; "LF GM" below says whether one exists.

## Designed values vs R-1 Table 1 (guidance, not enforced)

The R-1 Table 1 margins guided the PD design but are not pass/fail. The simulator deliberately places the *slosh-mode* gain-shift threshold below 2.0 (1.3 to 1.95) so that the gain-shift presets visibly destabilize the ~0.5 Hz slosh mode (teaching choice).

| Gain crossover [Hz] | 0.2 | 0.205 | 0.205 |

| HF GM frequency [Hz] | 0.525 | 0.513 | 0.513 |

## Per-mode gain-shift crossings

Every closed-loop pole pair is followed over GS 0.5 to 3 (step 0.01, 100 Hz ZOH loop). The pendulum participation is `r = L |dn| / |dp_quad|` from the eigenvector at the crossing. A crossing with r > 5 and frequency 0.4 to 0.65 Hz is the slosh mode; any other is a rigid-body mode.

| Mode | Axis | GS threshold | Frequency [Hz] | r |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| slosh | x | 1.344 | 0.513 | 17.6 |
| rigid-body | x | > 3 (no crossing) | - | - |
| slosh | y | 1.344 | 0.513 | 17.6 |
| rigid-body | y | > 3 (no crossing) | - | - |

**Damping shortfall (input for the pendulum-damping decision):** the predicted Fig 12 decay time exceeds 30 s on axis x, y. The GS threshold target was met first; the pendulum damping `k_damp` (currently 0.001) may need revising.
