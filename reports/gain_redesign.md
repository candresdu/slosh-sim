# Gain redesign: gains-only results (lever A)

Generated by `python -m slosh.design.redesign_report`. Everything below uses **PD gains only**
(inner attitude Kp, Kd and outer position Kp, Kd). The outer loop feeds back quad position and
velocity only; no pendulum-angle or mass-position signal is used, and no velocity-estimate filter
was added. The gain values are a *designed (reconstruction)*, not original R-1 values.

## Why the first-generation gains failed

The first design met a linear threshold of 1.757 but the wrong mode crossed: the outer-loop gain
crossover (0.48 Hz) sat on the slosh dipole, the lowest phase crossing was the rigid-body one at
1.04 Hz, and the slosh pole was phase-stabilized (its damping rose with the gain shift). The cause
was that the margin tool reported the largest pole magnitude of *any* mode as the threshold.

## What changed

* `slosh.design.margins` now follows every closed-loop pole pair over GS 0.5 to 3.0 (step 0.01) and
  reports, per crossing of |z| = 1, the GS, the frequency and the pendulum participation
  `r = L |dn| / |dp_quad|`. A crossing with r > 5 at 0.4 to 0.65 Hz is the slosh mode.
* `slosh.design.fit_gains` searches all four gains with a log-grid (6 points per gain) followed by
  Nelder-Mead on the eigenvalue-based objective. The target is a slosh-mode threshold in the hard
  band 1.3 to 1.95 (originally 1.6 to 1.9; the shipped design reaches 1.34, the previous one 1.61) with every other mode stable to
  GS 3.0, an inner PM of at least 45 deg (aim 50), clear growth at GS 2.0, and the soft Table 1
  shape. A last Nelder-Mead polish adds the nonlinear GS 2.0 run (AAC off) to the cost so that the
  theta envelope is back above 0.2 rad by t = 10 s.
* The inner loop became slower (crossover 0.8 Hz instead of 1.75 Hz). The extra lag at 0.5 Hz puts
  the rigid-body phase at about -180 deg near the slosh frequency while the outer gain crossover
  stays near 0.2 Hz, so the dipole loop crosses -180 deg just above the slosh pole.

## Which criteria gains alone can and cannot meet

* **Met by gains alone:** slosh-mode threshold in [1.3, 1.95] on both axes, rigid-body threshold
  above 3.0, inner PM at least 45 deg (crossover reported, below 1.5 Hz), stable at GS 1, and the
  nonlinear gate at GS 2.0 and 2.3 (envelope grows, stays at or above 0.2 rad over 10 to 60 s,
  dominant frequency 0.45 to 0.6 Hz, quad frequency outside 0.8 to 1.3 Hz). The §V.B ramp (AAC off) reaches 0.35 rad at the end of the 100 s run
  (see `reports/instability.md`); the previous gains peaked at 0.075 rad there.
* **Not met: Fig 12 decay (GS 1, 0.2 to 0.05 rad within 30 s).** The closed-loop slosh decay rate at
  GS 1 is about 0.006 1/s, essentially the passive pendulum damping (`k_damp` = 0.001). Over the
  whole search the slosh pole's real part moves only slightly with GS until close to the threshold,
  so any design with an identified slosh-mode threshold in [1.3, 1.95] has a GS 1 decay rate near the passive value (the
  best linear designs reached 0.008 1/s). The test stays an xfail (see the re-fit section below). Raising `k_damp` also shifts the threshold upwards, so the gains must be re-fitted
  afterwards (`fit_gains` reads `k_damp` from the parameter file).
* A hybridised design (slosh and rigid-body poles exchanging character, r about 5) had a faster GS 1
  decay (0.04 1/s) but a threshold of 1.45, outside the target, and an ambiguous mode identity.
  It was not used.

## Re-fit in the wider band and the k_damp step

The hard slosh-mode band was widened to [1.3, 1.95] (`SLOSH_THRESHOLD_RANGE`; the cost is flat in
[1.35, 1.88] so the objective rewards GS 1 slosh damping, not a high threshold). One re-fit of all
four gains (Nelder-Mead from the shipped gains, the first re-fit and a grid of 4096 points)
did **not** improve the GS 1 decay: every design whose crossing pole is the slosh mode
(participation r > 5, 0.45 to 0.6 Hz) has a decay rate of 0.005 to 0.0085 1/s at `k_damp` = 0.001
(the best was 0.0085 with threshold 1.79). The weight on the GS 1 damping reward was raised from 2
to 30 without a change. Only hybridised designs decay faster, and they fail the mode check:

`k_damp` = 0.005 is the smallest value in (0.002, 0.005, 0.01) that comes close
(Fig 12 envelope at 30 s: 0.12, 0.051 to 0.058, 0.012 rad); it still misses 0.05 rad, and the extra
passive damping pushes the slosh threshold to 1.93, leaving GS 2.0 almost neutral (growth about
0.006 1/s, so the GS 2.0 envelope stays near 0.18 to 0.22 rad). `k_damp` = 0.01 decays Fig 12 but the
threshold moves to 2.3. The shipped design is the lowest-threshold slosh-identified point of the wider band (13.02, 3.473 / 1.092, 0.8717; threshold 1.344, decay rate 0.0052 1/s at GS 1, growth 0.0265 1/s at GS 2.0), `k_damp` stays 0.001, and Fig 12 does **not** reach 0.05 rad within 30 s. It is the best design found: it meets every other target.

## Final bounded attempt (PM floor 40 deg, k_damp 0.003 to 0.007): no pass

Linear screen (`python -m slosh.design.final_search`, 20 min, 6 workers, started from the
k_damp = 0.005 rigid-stable design 11.77, 2.726 / 0.984, 0.757, inner PM floor 40 deg, no pendulum
feedback). A candidate had to have GS 1 slosh decay >= 0.046 1/s, GS 2.0 growth >= 0.025 1/s,
slosh identity (r > 5, 0.45 to 0.6 Hz), slosh threshold in [1.3, 1.95] on both axes, rigid-body
threshold >= 2.8 and inner PM >= 40 deg. Best point per k_damp (x and y axes are identical):

The design needs the sum (GS 1 decay + GS 2.0 growth) to be at least 0.071 1/s, i.e. a change of the
slosh-mode decay rate of about 0.07 1/s per unit GS. The best sum with every structural criterion
met (rigid threshold >= 2.8) is **0.047 1/s** (decay 0.0225, growth 0.0250, k_damp = 0.003), a gap
of about 0.024 1/s; the committed design reaches 0.032 1/s (0.0052 + 0.0265). Raising k_damp
raises the sum (0.055 to 0.058 at 0.004 to 0.006) only while the rigid-body mode crosses |z| = 1
below GS 2.8 (2.58 to 2.78); at 0.007 the sum reaches 0.088, but the quad's position mode (0.46 to
0.53 Hz) becomes unstable at GS 2.2 and the slosh pole is hybridised (r = 8). No design passed the
screen, so no nonlinear run was made and the fallback was taken: committed gains 13.02, 3.473 /
1.092, 0.8717 and k_damp = 0.001, inner PM floor back to 45 deg (aim 50). The Fig 12 criterion is
the replacement one in `reports/instability.md` ('Deviation from R-1').

## Velocity-estimate filter (lever B)

Not added. The criterion that gains alone cannot meet is the Fig 12 decay, which is limited by the
passive damping, not by the loop shape; a low-pass filter on the velocity estimate adds phase lag
and cannot create damping at GS 1 while keeping the threshold below 1.95. `control.velFilter`
is therefore not introduced, and the controller, fixtures and TypeScript core stay unchanged apart
from the regenerated fixture numbers.
