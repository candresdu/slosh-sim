## Story preset

Nominal (AAC Off, GS 1) -> gain shift (GS ramp to 2.3 over 1.5 s, AAC Monitor) -> AAC engaged (K_T applied as an immediate step, no ramp). Start like Fig 12.

- Dwell times: t_shift = 8 s; t_engage = 22 s (shift + 14 s); durationS = 52 s (D = 30 s after engagement).
- Trip delay in the story: 2.99 s after the shift (trip at 10.99 s).
- Envelope at shift / engage / end: 0.187 / 0.289 / 0.234 rad.
- Post-engage decay rate (fit of ln envelope over [t_engage + 5 s, end]): 0.01020 1/s; over the 400 s run: 0.00466 1/s.
- Time after engagement until envelope < 0.1 rad: 132.3 s (measured in a 400 s run; beyond the 30 s of the preset).

Trip delay when the same shift (ramp to 2.3 + Monitor, applied after 8 s of nominal flight) starts from a smaller amplitude (kick of theta0 with phiDot scaled proportionally; the UI narration uses these to decide when to suggest a Kick):

| theta0 [rad] | envelope at shift [rad] | trip delay after shift [s] |
|---|---|---|
| 0.2 | 0.187 | 2.99 |
| 0.13 | 0.122 | 4.07 |
| 0.05 | 0.047 | 17.59 |

The post-engage criterion is the relaxed one (growth stops, positive decay rate, envelope <= 0.9 x its value at engagement), see `reports/aac_tuning.md`, 'Known deviations'. Tests: `test_preset_story`, `test_clear_schedule` in `python/tests/test_presets.py`.
