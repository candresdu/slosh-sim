## Conclusions

- All acceptance checks pass for the committed parameters.
- Frequency: with AAC off the dominant theta frequency is 0.519 Hz (GS 2.0) and 0.525 Hz (GS 2.3), the ~0.5 Hz slosh mode; the quad-x frequency is 0.517 / 0.520 Hz, locked to the same mode (the quad is driven by the swinging mass), not the ~1 Hz rigid-body position mode of run 1.
- Growth: fig13 envelope 0.255 rad at 10 s, 0.634 at 30 s, 1.160 at 60 s; GS 2.0: 0.216, 0.347, 0.598. V.B (AAC off) peaks at 0.371 rad before 100 s.
- Configuration (b): a smaller attMax lowers the amplitude where growth stops; the saturation columns show how often a clamp is active.
- Configuration (c) is for information only. The committed parameters keep latencyMs = 0 and noise disabled; the instability does not need either.
- Fig 12 (GS 1) envelope: 0.1847 rad at 10 s, 0.1640 at 30 s, 0.1373 at 60 s; linear GS 1 slosh-mode decay rate 0.0052 1/s.
- Pendulum damping and gains: the decision sequence is recorded in `reports/gain_redesign.md` (sections 'Re-fit in the wider band and the k_damp step' and 'Final bounded attempt'). No design passes the linear screen, so `k_damp` stays 0.001 and the committed gains are the wider-band re-fit (threshold 1.344); the Fig 12 criterion is the fallback one (see 'Deviation from R-1').

## Deviation from R-1

R-1 shows Fig 12 (GS 1) decaying quickly. Here Fig 12 decays only at about the passive rate of the pendulum (about 0.005 1/s at `k_damp` = 0.001; the envelope falls slowly and does not reach 0.05 rad within 30 s or 60 s). Two reasons:

- PD-only gains cannot give both enough slosh damping at GS 1 and enough growth at GS 2.0. Fig 12 needs about 0.046 1/s of decay at GS 1 and the V.B ramp needs about 0.025 1/s of growth at GS 2.0, so the slosh-mode decay rate must change by about 0.07 1/s per unit of GS. The bounded search (`reports/gain_redesign.md`, 'Final bounded attempt') reached at most 0.047 1/s with the other criteria met (gap about 0.024 1/s); the committed design changes it by about 0.032 1/s per unit of GS.
- Pendulum feedback is excluded: the slosh stands for unobservable, unmodeled dynamics, so the controller never measures the pendulum angle, rate or mass position.

The replacement criterion for Fig 12 is: the 2 s sliding-max envelope decreases, env(60 s) < env(30 s) < env(10 s), and the linear GS 1 slosh-mode decay rate is positive.

### Conclusions

- **Not all acceptance checks pass.** The linear instability exists (the probe above grows without bound when attitude is not clamped), but its frequency is the ~1 Hz quad position mode (`reports/margins.md`: unstable pole at 6.5 rad/s), not the 0.5 Hz slosh mode. The pendulum carries only 3 % of the mass and is not fed back, so it is merely driven by the quad.
- With attMax = 0.35 rad the unstable quad mode saturates into a bounded limit cycle (quad |x| about 0.1 m). The theta envelope of fig13 then decays from 0.24 rad at 10 s to 0.19 rad at 60 s, and the V.B ramp (AAC off) peaks at 0.16 rad before 100 s. Changing attMax between 0.2 and 0.8 rad does not fix this (see the table), because attMax only changes the limit-cycle amplitude of the quad, not which mode is unstable.
- Fixing it needs a design change in the gain design (task: PD gain design): the gain-shift instability must sit at the slosh frequency (HF gain-margin frequency near 0.5 Hz, as in R-1 Table 1), for example by putting the pendulum angle in the outer loop, so that growth appears in theta and the x-to-y migration is carried by the mass.
- Configuration (b): a smaller attMax lowers the amplitude where growth stops; the saturation columns show how often a clamp is active.
- Configuration (c) is for information only. The committed parameters keep latencyMs = 0 and noise disabled.
- Pendulum damping: Fig 12 decays below 0.05 rad within 30 s with k_damp = 0.001 N m s, so no change to `fit_gains` or `k_damp` was made; `reports/margins.md` is unchanged.
